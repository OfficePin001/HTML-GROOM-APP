IN THIS CLASS WE WILL LEARN ABOUT Headings, PARAGRAPHS AND LIST


What is heading?

Heading in html are block-level elements which usually contains a title or main topic of a certain content. We make use of the HEADING TAGS (<h1> to <h6>).

Example

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
   <meta charset="UTF-8">
   <title>Page title</title>
</head>
<body>

<h1> Hello World! </h1>
<h2> Hello World! </h2>
<h3> Hello World! </h3>
<h4> Hello World! </h4>
<h5> Hello World! </h5>
<h6> Hello World! </h6>

</body>
</html>

OUTPUT



Hello World!

Hello World!

Hello World!

Hello World!

Hello World!
Hello World!

You noticed that the text output reduces as the number of the heading increase, this is to say that the smaller the number the bigger the text and vice versa. Also we can style our headings to make it look more interactive.

Example

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
   <meta charset="UTF-8">
   <title>Page title</title>
</head>
<body>

<h1 style="text-align:center ;"> aligned to the center </h1>
<h2 style="text-align:left ;"> aligned to the left </h2>
<h3 style="text-align:right ;"> aligned to the right </h3>
<h4 style="color:gold ;"> I am colored gold </h4>
<h5 style="color:blue ;"> I am colored blue </h5>
<h6 style="color:red ;"> I am colored red </h6>

</body>
</html>

OUTPUT



aligned to the center

aligned to the left

aligned to the right

I am colored gold

I am colored blue
I am colored red

You can also nest the heading tag in other elements such as <i>, <u>, etc. You will learn more about it in the next class TEXT FORMATTING.

Example

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
   <meta charset="UTF-8">
   <title>Page title</title>
</head>
<body>

<i> <h1> Hello World! </h1> </i>
<u> <h2> Hello World! </h2> </u>

</body>
</html>

OUTPUT



Hello World!

Hello World!


We move to paragraph

What is a paragraph?

Paragraph (<p></p>) in html is used to group sentences, it can also be styled e.g color, alignment, font size, font family, weight and so on. It is a content element tag and a block level element by default.

Example

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
   <meta charset="UTF-8">
   <title>Page title</title>
</head>
<body>

<p> I am paragraph by default that have not been styled. </p>
<p style="text-align:center ;"> I am a paragraph that has be aligned to the center. </p>
<p style="font-size:20px ;"> I am a paragraph whose font size has been increased to 20 pixels. </p>
<p style="font-weight:bold ;"> I am a paragraph that is weighed bold. </p>
<p style="font-family:'Brush script MT';"> I am a paragraph from the cursive family Brush script MT. </p>
<p style="color:brown;"> I am a paragraph with the color brown. </p>

</body>
</html>

OUTPUT



I am paragraph by default that have not been styled.

I am a paragraph that has be aligned to the center.

I am a paragraph whose font size has been increased to 20 pixels.

I am a paragraph that is weighed bold.

I am a paragraph from the cursive family Brush script MT.

I am a paragraph with the color brown.


To end it we will learn about LIST in html.

List is the method of grouping items with or without order. The list (<li></li>) tag can be nested inside an ordered list (<ol></ol>) tag or an unordered list (<ul></ul>) tag, when the list tag is nest inside an ordered list tag it gives the items numbers in order from 1 to the required item but when the list tag is nested in an unordered list tag it creates a bullet point listing to the items.

Example

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
   <meta charset="UTF-8">
   <title>Page title</title>
</head>
<body>

<p> An ordered listing </p>
<ol>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
</ol>

<p> An unordered listing </p>
<ul>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
   <li> Hello World! </li>
</ul>

</body>
</html>

OUTPUT



An ordered listing.

  1. The green apple
  2. The yellow banana
  3. The black berry
  4. The red water melon
  5. The brown coconut

An unordered listing.

  • The green apple
  • The yellow banana
  • The black berry
  • The red water melon
  • The brown coconut
