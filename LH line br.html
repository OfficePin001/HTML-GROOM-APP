What is line break?

Line break in html gives an extra line in a text, it is an empty element <br />

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
   <meta charset="UTF-8">
   <title>Page title</title>
</head>
<body>

Row row row your boat,<br/> gently down the stream, <br/>merrily merrily merrily merrily, <br/>life is but a dream.

</body>
</html>

OUTPUT



Row row row your boat,
gently down the stream,
merrily merrily merrily merrily,
life is but a dream.

What is horizontal rule?

Horizontal rule is a line that cross across the page horizontally.

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
   <meta charset="UTF-8">
   <title>Page title</title>
</head>
<body>

<hr/>
Row row row your boat,<br/> gently down the stream, <br/>merrily merrily merrily merrily, <br/>life is but a dream.
<hr/>

</body>
</html>

OUTPUT



Row row row your boat,
gently down the stream,
merrily merrily merrily merrily,
life is but a dream.

What is non breakable space?

Non breakable space (&nbsp) is a character entity that give a space in the browser without moving in to the next line. This character entities are not surrounded by angle brackets they are declared with "&", "&#" or "&#x".

The "&" is used for name encoding (&entity_name), the "&#" is used for decimal encoding (&#entity_number) and the "&#x" is used for hexadecimal (&#xentity_hexadecimal).

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
   <meta charset="UTF-8">
   <title>Page title</title>
</head>
<body>

<!-- These are entity names --> I am &nbsp apart
<br/> &copy I am copyright
<br/> &reg I am registered trademark<br/>

<!-- These are entity numbers -->
I am &#160 apart
<br/> &#169 I am copyright
<br/> &#174 I am registered trademark<br/>

<!-- These are entity hexadecimal -->
I am &#x00A0 apart
<br/> &#x00A9 I am copyright
<br/> &#x00AE I am registered trademark<br/>

</body>
</html>

OUTPUT



I am   apart
© I am copyright
® I am registered trademark
